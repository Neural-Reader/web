Narrate audiobook chapters with text to speech

Start with a manuscript you have the rights to narrate. Choose one voice for the book, then export chapters separately so you can revise a passage without rebuilding the whole project.

Turn a manuscript into chapter audio

  1. Clean up the manuscript for listening

    Keep headings you want spoken. Remove page numbers, running headers, and footnotes you don’t want in the narration.

  2. Record a reference passage

    Choose a cloud voice and language in Settings → Voice. Tap +, choose Read aloud → Text, paste a passage with dialogue and character names, then tap Read. Listen for pronunciation and keep a note of the voice and language you chose.

  3. Generate and name each chapter

    Use the same settings throughout the book. Split a long chapter at a natural pause if needed for your plan’s text limit. In the Library, rename the recordings by chapter and part, then use Export to save the MP3s.

  4. Listen to the joins before assembly

    Check the end and start of each part for repeated lines, missing words, or abrupt pauses. Assemble the chapter files in your audio editor and check the finished audio against your distributor’s requirements.

Keep a record of pronunciation edits

Keep a short pronunciation list alongside the manuscript. If a name needs a phonetic spelling, make that change in your narration copy and reuse it in later chapters. Keep the original manuscript for publication.

Confirm where you can publish

Check whether your intended distributor accepts synthetic narration before generating the full book. Use a manuscript you have the rights to narrate and publish. For public or commercial use, generate and export your MP3s during a paid billing period.
