Frequently asked questions

Can I try Neural Reader for free?

Yes. Try cloud reading as a guest with a short passage. A free account has a higher per-text limit, and paid plans offer larger monthly word allowances. See the current limits and local prices under Subscription in the app.

What can I import?

Neural Reader can read PDFs, EPUBs, text, Word and PowerPoint files, web articles, and extracted text from photos. You can also paste text directly.

Can I share something directly to Neural Reader?

Yes. On iPhone, iPad, or Android, open the Share menu in an app that supports sharing and select Neural Reader. You can send an article link, a supported document, or photos of printed pages. Choose Read aloud and check the extracted text before you listen. See how to share an article.

Can I choose the reading voice?

Yes. Preview cloud voices here, then open Settings in the app to choose a reading language and voice for your next recording. Play voice samples.

Which languages and voices are available?

The current catalogue has 155 language and regional choices and 1,044 cloud voice options. Each language has its own voice list; US English currently has 162. These choices are for reading. The app’s menus are in English. Browse all languages and voices.

Can I read part of a PDF?

Yes. Choose Range after selecting the PDF, then enter the first and last page you want. Check the extracted text before you tap Read.

Can I ask questions about a document?

Yes. Choose Ask anything when adding a source. Ask for a summary, request an explanation of a term, or follow up on an answer.

Does Neural Reader transcribe speech?

Yes. Live Transcribe captures speech as text that you can export or share.

What happens to the text or files I import?

Neural Reader uses online processing to extract text from uploaded files, generate audio with cloud voices, and answer document questions. Read the privacy policy.
