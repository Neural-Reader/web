Read & listen

Turn a saved article into something you can listen to on your commute. The same flow works for PDFs, ebooks, and scanned pages.

  1. Share or import

    Choose Neural Reader in your browser’s Share menu, or add a file, link, or photo in the app.

  2. Listen at your pace

    Choose Read aloud, check the text, and tap Read. Adjust the speed as you listen.

  3. Pick up later

    Reopen the saved recording in your library to resume where you stopped on the same device.

Common questions

What can I import?

Neural Reader can read PDFs, EPUBs, text, Word and PowerPoint files, web articles, and extracted text from photos. You can also paste text directly.

Can I use an exported MP3 in a video or audiobook?

Yes. You can publish or monetize MP3s you generate and export during a paid billing period, with any voice available for MP3 export. Free and trial exports are for personal, non-commercial use. Read what the license covers.

What happens to the text or files I import?

Neural Reader uses online processing to extract text from uploaded files, generate audio with cloud voices, and answer document questions. Read the privacy policy.
