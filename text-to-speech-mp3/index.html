Convert text to speech and export an MP3

Keep a copy of your narrated document or share it with another app. Export is available from the recording’s menu in your Neural Reader library.

Create and save the recording

  1. Choose a cloud voice

    In Settings → Voice, choose a voice without the “Offline Voice” label. Stay connected while you generate the recording and export it.

  2. Add your text

    Tap + and choose Read aloud, then tap Text to paste a passage or select another source such as a file. Review the text and tap Read.

  3. Export the audio item

    After generation finishes, open the audio item’s menu in your library and tap Export. Export uses the file type of the item you select, so choose the audio recording for an MP3.

  4. Save or share the MP3

    Choose a local destination or another app from your device’s share menu. Open the saved copy to check it plays before going offline.

Check for a saved audio recording

Device voices read text in the offline player and do not create a recording in the library. Select a cloud voice and generate the text again if you need a file to export.

Generation can be limited by your plan and available word balance. For a long source, pick the relevant section and check the finished audio before exporting it.

Keep the files easy to recognize

Rename a recording in your library before exporting to give the MP3 a useful name. If you export several parts of a document, include the chapter title or page range in each name.
