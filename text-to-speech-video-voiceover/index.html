Create a video voiceover with text to speech

Turn your script into voiceover clips on iPhone, iPad, or Android. Working one scene at a time makes it easier to fit the narration to your video and replace a line after the cut changes.

Go from script to editing timeline

  1. Split your script by scene

    Give each scene its own short block of narration. Leave stage directions and timestamps out of the text you want read aloud.

  2. Test the voice on a tricky line

    In Settings → Voice, choose a cloud voice. Tap +, choose Read aloud → Text, and paste a line with a name, acronym, or number. Tap Read and listen before generating the full voiceover.

  3. Export one MP3 per scene

    Generate each block with the same voice. Use the recording’s menu in your Library to rename it (such as “01 Intro” or “02 Demo”), then tap Export and choose where to save or share it.

  4. Place the clips in your video editor

    Import the MP3s, line each one up with the action, and adjust pauses and music in your editor. If the picture changes, replace only the clip for that scene.

Write for the timing of the scene

Try the clip against the rough cut. If instructions run past the moment they describe, give the action more time or shorten the line and regenerate it. Changing playback speed in the app changes how you hear the preview. Adjust the exported clip in your editor if you need a different pace.

Before you publish or monetize a video

Use a script you wrote or have permission to narrate and publish. Generate and export the voiceover during a paid billing period, and check your video platform’s rules for synthetic narration and disclosure.
