Data Deletion

You can delete your account and account data directly in the Neural Reader app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap Confirm.
View the in-app screenshot

Need help?

If you can’t sign in or need help with a deletion request, contact Support.
