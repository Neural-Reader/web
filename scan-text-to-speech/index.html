Scan printed text and read it aloud

Use your phone to turn a few pages of a textbook, a handout, or printed notes into something you can listen to.

Take a photo, then listen

  1. Add photos of the pages

    Tap +, choose Read aloud, then tap Images. Take photos with the camera or choose them from your device.

  2. Put pages in reading order

    Drag the images into order and tap Continue. Review Extracted text to make sure each page is represented.

  3. Correct the text and listen

    Fix missing words or awkward breaks, then tap Read. Follow the text on screen or listen hands-free.

Get a clearer scan

Lay each page flat, light it evenly, and photograph it straight on. Try one page per image so the text is easier to check in order.

Tables, sidebars, and multi-column notes may read out of sequence. Compare the extracted text with the page and fix the order before generating audio.
