How to choose a voice in the app ↓See the language picker ↗Download the app ↓

Find a voice for your next recording

  1. Choose your reading language

    Open Settings and choose Language under Read aloud. Select the language of your text before choosing a voice.

  2. Listen to voice samples

    Return to Settings and open Voice. Tap the play button beside a name to hear a sample. For Neural Reader’s cloud voices, choose a name without the “Offline Voice” label.

  3. Select a voice and try your own text

    Tap the voice’s row; a checkmark confirms your choice. Tap +, choose Read aloud, then Text. Paste a short passage and tap Read while connected.

  4. Set a comfortable pace

    Open Audio speed in the player. Try a slower pace for an unfamiliar topic or a faster pace for a quick review. Use the same voice for a longer source when you’re happy with the result.

Try a passage you know

A sample helps you choose a voice; your own text helps you decide whether it suits the material. Try a paragraph with the names, terms, and sentence lengths you expect in the full document.

Cloud voices create recordings you can save to your library and export as MP3s. Device voices labeled “Offline Voice” are also available if you want to compare how the same passage sounds.

Keep your choice for new recordings

Selecting a voice changes the voice used for new audio. It does not change recordings you have already generated. If you change the reading language, choose your voice again.

Once you’ve found a voice you enjoy, bring in a PDF, an EPUB, a web article, or scanned pages. Check the extracted text before generating a longer recording; your plan and word balance affect how much you can create.
